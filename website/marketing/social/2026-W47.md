## 5. English

If your dog hates summer walks, the sidewalk itself might be the problem more than the heat in the air — dark pavement can get hot enough to burn paw pads, especially in the few hours after peak sun. Press the back of your hand on the sidewalk for five seconds: too hot for you means too hot for your dog. Early morning and after sunset beat a midday walk every time.
