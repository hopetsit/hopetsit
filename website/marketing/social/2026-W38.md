## 4. Langue du jour

Austin dog owners: finding a sitter you can actually trust doesn't have to be a gamble. Expect $30-55 a day for in-home sitting and $15-25 for a single walk, a bit more close to downtown and South Congress. Always do a short meet-and-greet first, never pay in full cash upfront, and look for live GPS tracking so "he had a great walk" comes with an actual route attached. We wrote up the full guide, no pitch, just what to check:
