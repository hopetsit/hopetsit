## 5. English

If your dog hates winter walks, it might be the sidewalk salt more than the cold — ice-melt chemicals can dry out or crack paw pads, and dogs that lick their paws afterward can end up with an upset stomach. A damp towel by the door, shorter and more frequent walks, and watching your dog instead of the thermometer make a real difference.
